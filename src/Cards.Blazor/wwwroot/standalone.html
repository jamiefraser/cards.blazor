Cards.Blazor Web Component

How to Use

This demonstrates the Cards.Blazor library running as a Blazor WebAssembly web component.

  • Click DEAL to deal 5 cards to each hand
  • Click the deck (left stack) to draw a card to your hand
  • Click a card in your hand (bottom) that matches the suit or rank of the top discard pile card (right stack) to play it
Cards.Blazor was created as a C#/.NET port of cards.js and is licensed under the MIT license. The card images were created by Nicu Buculei and are in the public domain.
